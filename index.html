About Me

Hello! My name is Itumeleng Ramashamole, a passionate university student based in Maseru, Lesotho. I am currently studying Web Technology and Multimedia, where I’ve gained hands-on experience in web design, digital storytelling, and online publishing. I enjoy blending creativity with technology to create engaging digital content, whether it's through websites, podcasts, or broadcast media.

Outside the classroom, I’m actively involved in student journalism and broadcasting. I have covered live campus events, hosted educational radio shows, and produced youth-centered interviews that focus on innovation, community impact, and student voices. My work aims to reflect the vibrancy and resilience of young people in Lesotho.

I am also a dedicated soccer player, competing in inter-varsity matches and using the sport as a way to stay disciplined, healthy, and connected with my peers. Soccer has helped shape my leadership, focus, and ability to thrive under pressure—skills I also apply to my media projects and tech learning.

This portfolio showcases my growing journey as a communicator, developer, and athlete. I am excited to continue building my skills and contributing to innovative media and technology in Lesotho and beyond.
